# Suggested Gentle Alert Phrases

This file contains starter friendly/gentle voice alert phrases for MindMend Guardian. Contributors can add wording variants and translations.

Examples:
1. "Hey, are you okay? I noticed something—please check your surroundings."  
2. "This area is off-limits. Please return to a safe place."  
3. "Warning: Unexpected approach detected nearby. Stay with a trusted adult."  
4. "If you need help, press the red button or call someone you trust."  
5. "Friendly reminder: This space is private—please move away."  
6. "I sensed unusual activity—please stay close to home or a guardian."  
7. "If you feel unsafe, find a trusted person immediately."  
8. "Alert: Please verify who is nearby before proceeding."  
9. "This device is protecting this area. Please leave if you are not authorized."  
10. "If you are lost or need help, use the nearest safe contact method."
