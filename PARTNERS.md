# Partnership Opportunities

## Michigan MindMend Inc. / HumaniCare AI

Michigan MindMend Inc. is building **HumaniCare AI**, a privacy-first, local-first AI safety ecosystem for youth safety, family wellness, healthcare access, and community resilience.

**Phase 1 is complete:** the public portfolio now has clear positioning, core repos, safety boundaries, documentation, and early working prototypes.  
**Phase 2 is underway:** demo proof, production hardening, local model paths, edge deployment notes, and real-world partner feedback.

---

## What We Are Building

HumaniCare AI is an umbrella system connecting:

- **OpenClaw Empathy Anchor** — empathy and safety response module
- **MindMend Guardian** — youth/family safety workflows
- **TrustLayer** — LLM safety gateway and privacy middleware
- **MindMend Vault / HumaniCare API** — local-first support, triage, and audit-log scaffold
- **Journal Coach / Link Sentinel / Crisis Layer** — planned or emerging support modules

The goal is not to replace clinicians, parents, emergency services, or trusted humans.

The goal is to build privacy-first support infrastructure that helps communities use AI more safely.

---

## What We Are Not Claiming

HumaniCare AI is not:

- a replacement for licensed professionals
- a diagnostic system
- emergency services
- a medical device
- clinically validated yet
- HIPAA compliant unless formally reviewed and implemented in a compliant deployment

We use careful language: **privacy-first, local-first, clinician-informed, safety-focused, human-in-the-loop.**
